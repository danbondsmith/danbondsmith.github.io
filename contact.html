I acknowledge Hawaiʻi as an Indigenous space. I recognize that Her Majesty Queen Liliʻuokalani yielded the Hawaiian Kingdom under duress and protest to the United States. As a settler, I seek to support the varied strategies that the Indigenous peoples of Hawaiʻi are using to protect their land and their communities, and I commit to dedicating time and resources to working in solidarity.
