Take part in research

To help fill gaps in public information, I’m starting a community data collection initiative on special education in Hawaiʻi’s public schools. It will ask parents, caregivers, and others in the school community about their schools and their experiences. The results will be available to the public, for example as a user-friendly searchable database, maps, and charts. Taking part is always voluntary, and participants’ confidentiality is protected.

No study is open yet. To hear when one opens, sign up for research updates below.

Research updates

I send occasional updates about my research on special education and disability in Hawaiʻi. They cover:

  • New data, reports and findings
  • New studies that families can take part in
  • Resources for families

I’ll use your email address only to send these updates. It is stored securely and I’m the only person who can access it.
